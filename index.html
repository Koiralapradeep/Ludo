Game Manual & Rules

Objective

The objective of Ludo is to move your four tokens from your base yard around the outer track, down your color runway, and into the center Home triangle before your opponents.

Rules of Play

  • Roll the dice on your turn. You must roll a **6** to move a token from its Base yard to the Starting tile.
  • Rolling a **6** grants an extra roll. (Rolling three 6s in a row forfeits the turn).
  • Tokens travel clockwise around the perimeter path.
  • If you land on a cell occupied by an opponent's token, their token is **captured** and returned to their base yard. A capture grants you an extra roll!
  • Tiles marked with a **star** are safe zones. Tokens cannot be captured while resting on safe zones.
  • Getting a token into the home triangle also awards an extra roll.

Install on Mobile

Since this is a Progressive Web App, you can install it on your Android or iOS homescreen! Click the menu button in your browser and select **"Install App"** or **"Add to Home Screen"** to launch it fullscreen without browser frames.
